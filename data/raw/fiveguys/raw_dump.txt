Nutrition & Allergen Guide
USA
Your safety matters to us. Five Guys works hard to identify ingredients that may cause allergic reactions, and our allergen chart shows which of the nine major food allergens (i.e., milk, eggs, fish,
crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans, and sesame) we know or reasonably should know are used in each menu item. Please review the allergen chart before you order. Our food
is made to order by hand in kitchens that handle the noted allergens, so there is always a risk of cross-contact between ingredients. In addition, while we work with our suppliers to stay informed of
changes to their ingredients and formulations, these can change, and such changes may not always be reflected immediately. For these reasons, Five Guys and its franchisees cannot assume liability for
adverse reactions to foods eaten, or to items you may come into contact with, while at any Five Guys restaurant. We also share nutritional information on this site. These figures are based on values from
our suppliers, published resources, industry-standard software, and accredited lab testing, and they follow federal rounding and other applicable rules. Because every order is made by hand, serving
sizes can vary slightly, which may affect a product’s nutrition values, so Five Guys cannot guarantee that this information is exact for every item in every restaurant. As a guide, a 2,000 calorie daily diet
allows about 20 grams of saturated fat and 2,300 milligrams of sodium.
